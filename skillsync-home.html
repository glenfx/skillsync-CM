How SkillSync CM works.

From raw job data to actionable career intelligence in four stages.

1
Data Collection
Real job postings gathered from Cameroonian boards, LinkedIn, and company pages — titles, skills, locations, and salaries extracted.
2
Processing and Cleaning
Raw data normalized, duplicates removed, suspicious listings flagged, and skills extracted into structured categories.
3
Intelligence Generation
Algorithms surface trends, rank skills by demand frequency, map regional concentrations, and generate market insights.
4
Personalized Guidance
Submit your CV, compare your skills to the market, and receive precise guidance on exactly where to focus your growth.
